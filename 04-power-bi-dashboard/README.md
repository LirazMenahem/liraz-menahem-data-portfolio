# Project 4: Bank Loan Portfolio Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
A multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes the bank's loan and credit portfolio.
It gives executive leadership and risk managers a data-driven view of clients, loan products,
risk exposure and branch / banker performance.

## Business questions
The dashboard has four pages, each built around a core domain:

**1. Client Analysis**
- What is the demographic and economic profile of the client base?
- How are clients distributed across age groups, income brackets and banking type, and which segment is the most valuable?

**2. Loans Analysis**
- What are the main characteristics and volume trends across credit products?
- What are the total loan volume and average loan size, and how does credit demand change over time?

**3. Risk & Credit Analysis**
- What is the risk profile of the portfolio, and where is default exposure concentrated?
- What is the late-loans rate (`lateLoansPCT`), how strongly does credit score correlate with late payments, and is the bank within its target risk thresholds?

**4. Branch & Banker Analysis**
- Which branches and loan officers are the most productive and keep the healthiest portfolios?
- How are total credit and portfolio quality distributed across branches and bankers?
